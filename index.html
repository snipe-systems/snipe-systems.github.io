02

How everything here runs

Four rules apply to every unit, every post, and every line of research. They are not aspirations — they are the publishing checklist.

RULE 01

Numbers or nothing

Nothing ships without data in it. A market post with no numbers in it is an empty post, and empty posts don't go out — the slot gets real data or a verified fact, never filler.

RULE 02

Verify before it counts

Every substantive result — a backtest, a statistic, a claim in an article — gets checked independently before it reaches an audience. Trading is probabilities: report the measured edge and its significance, not a verdict the data can't carry.

RULE 03

Nothing repeats

No repeated stats, angles, or phrasings across the channels, ever. When a scheduled piece collides with something already published, it gets replaced with a fresh one — the slot is never left empty and never filled with a rerun.

RULE 04

Score the misses in public

Calls get revisited and graded, including the wrong ones. A scanner alert, a backtest, an article's thesis — each is a record that can be checked later, and the checking is part of the product.
