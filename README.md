# Snipe Systems

A holding company building trading tools, voice execution software, and money media. Follow the money.

## Live site

https://snipe-systems.github.io/

## What lives here

- **Snipe Flow** — money media on X (@snipe010) and Instagram (@snipe0100)
- **The Desk** — options-flow scanner for SPY and QQQ
- **The Lab** — verification unit: every claim backtested before it ships
- **Voice Desk** — voice-controlled trade execution software (in development)
- **Ventures** — new experiments as they launch

## Library

Articles, stories, and research reports live on the site under Library — entry 001 is the voice trading agents article.
